# Product Genome Studio

For product managers, designers, and teams designing a new product flow who are tired of solving the same problem with screenshot collections and a meeting: which proven interaction pattern actually wins at each step of the customer journey, and does it stay consistent with everything around it? Product Genome Studio turns that into an interactive, scorable exercise. Pick an industry and a journey stage, choose from patterns pulled from companies that already do it well, and see the choice previewed live on a phone screen with real-time consistency scoring and automatic conflict flagging (e.g. an emotional tone choice colliding with a transactional checkout flow).

## How to use

Live: [productgenomestudio.lovable.app](https://productgenomestudio.lovable.app)

Covers 12 industries, 240 patterns, and 60 funnel stages, with three preset design goals and customizable industries.

## How it's built

React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, built entirely on Lovable (no hand-written code), with Claude (via Cowork mode) and 11Labs assisting on content, and Novus for analytics. Client-side rendered and deployed.

Submitted to Mind the Product's *World Product Day: Everyone Ships Now* competition (June 2026).
